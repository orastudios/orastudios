What's coming

Two rooms. One standard.

Ōra was built around a simple idea: your workout should fit your day.

Some days you'll want the energy of a heated mat class. Other days, you'll want the precision of the reformer. With two rooms under one roof, you don't have to choose between studios — just how you want to move.

Whichever room you walk into, you can expect the same thoughtful instruction, warm welcome, and attention to every detail.

The rooms

Heat and springs

Room 01

The heated room

Built for movement that leaves you feeling lighter than when you walked in.

Our heated mat room uses infrared panels instead of forced hot air, warming your body more evenly for a comfortable, deep sweat. Expect a mix of classical and contemporary Pilates with props when the class calls for them.

Bring your own mat. Towels and cold water are always waiting for you.

Room 02

The reformer room

Precision. Strength. Control.

Nine reformers. Small classes. Instructors who actually know your name and have time to coach you.

Whether it's your very first class or you've spent years on the carriage, you'll find classes that meet you where you are and keep you coming back.

Frequently asked

Questions, answered

What is heated mat Pilates?
Heated mat Pilates combines classical and contemporary Pilates in a room warmed by infrared panels. Instead of heating the air, infrared warms your body directly for a comfortable, deep sweat and greater mobility.
Where is ora located?
1459 SW Gatlin Blvd, Unit 12, Gatlin Square, Port St. Lucie, FL 34953. Just off I-95 with free parking right outside the studio.
When do you open?
We're opening Fall 2026. Join the Founding List to be the first to hear about memberships, pricing, class schedules, and opening day.
Do you serve Tradition, St. Lucie West & Stuart?
Yes. We're just off I-95 in Gatlin Square — about 10 minutes from Tradition and St. Lucie West, and around 25 minutes from Stuart.
I've never done Pilates. Can I start here?
Absolutely. Whether it's your first class or you've been doing Pilates for years, our instructors are there to help you move with confidence and get the most out of every class.
How do memberships work?
We'll offer memberships and single drop-in classes. Founding Members will receive our best pricing before we open, with full details announced closer to launch.
What should I bring?
Grip socks are required for reformer classes. For heated mat classes, please bring your own mat. Bring a water bottle, and we'll have a chilled towel waiting for you after class. If you forget anything, we'll have studio essentials available for purchase in our retail shop.
